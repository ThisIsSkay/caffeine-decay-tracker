How this estimate works

Each completed intake is treated as a separate dose and decays independently using a one-compartment, first-order model. The simple mg-remaining view assumes the dose is available immediately at the recorded intake time.

The 3–8 hour values are a practical adult sensitivity reference, not a confidence interval and not a personalized biological range.

Estimates parent caffeine remaining under the selected model; it is not a blood measurement.
